## What I believe

Technology only creates value when people actually use it.

The hardest part of transformation is rarely the technology itself. More often, it's helping organizations change how they work, make decisions, and create new habits. Tools are just tools. Real impact comes from understanding people, designing for adoption, and building solutions that fit into the reality of day-to-day operations.

Having worked extensively with industries that were not digitally mature, I've learned that successful innovation requires equal parts product thinking, change management, stakeholder alignment, and patience.

AI is no different. The opportunity isn't simply to add AI to existing processes, but to rethink how work gets done while bringing people along on the journey.
